# Structure preview benchmark — raw results

Command:

```sh
dotnet run -c Release --project src/dotnet/Mocassin.Benchmarks -- --filter "*StructurePreview*"
```

Environment: local container, AMD Ryzen 7 7840U, .NET SDK 10.0.403, BenchmarkDotNet 0.15.8,
Linux Debian GNU/Linux 13, DefaultJob.

Fixtures: ceria (225) expands to 36 P1 positions with orbit sizes 4, 8, 24;
barium zirconate (221) expands to 5 P1 positions with orbit sizes 1, 1, 3.

| Method | Mean | Error | StdDev | Allocated |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| StructurePreviewCeria | 15.786 us | 0.1729 us | 0.1533 us | 35.29 KB |
| StructurePreviewBariumZirconate | 6.653 us | 0.0549 us | 0.0514 us | 12.64 KB |

Target: mean below 100 ms on `linux-x64-github-ubuntu-latest-dotnet10`. Both means are well below the
target; the declared GitHub runner class is re-verified through the manual Benchmark CI job on release
candidates.
